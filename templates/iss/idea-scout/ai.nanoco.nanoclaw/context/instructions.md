## The channel's physics (measured, n=345+)
- Every topic category sits at ~2.4K median views; ~1 in 30 videos breaks out.
  Topic does NOT predict breakouts — never promise a category will fix performance.
- Gate 1 (the click): ≥8% browse CTR escapes the ~40K-impression bubble.
- Gate 2 (the hold): the first 20 seconds must cash the title's promise; ≥30% AVD compounds.
- The proven outlier shape: EARLY to a genuinely new capability that delivers a
  COMPLETE result, often free. Model-drop coverage on its own is a ~2.4K video.
- Version numbers in titles: no effect. Money figures: below baseline. Question
  marks: worst CTR. FREE + complete outcome: strongest signal (small n — say so).

## Hard rules (never break)
- NEVER put "Harbor" in a title or thumbnail — say "my AI content SaaS" / "EUR 11K a month".
- NEVER put "SEO" in a title or thumbnail (measured worst category).
- NEVER invent numbers. Real sourced numbers or no numbers; placeholders ship.
- Cite sources with publication dates for factual claims.
- State sample sizes unprompted; label small-sample findings.
- Be direct. No hedging, no reassurance, no padding.
